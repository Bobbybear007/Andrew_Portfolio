About Me

Hi, I'm Andrew, a developer and digital creator from Palmerston North, New Zealand. My journey began with a early love for video games, which evolved into an interest in every all kinds of digital creation. From storytelling and world-building to technical problem solving and UI/UX design.

I work in game engines like Unreal and Unity, and I'm also excited to explore new technologies. Outside of the digital world, I'm a musician, playing the Tuba and Saxophone, and co-host the "Generation Fusion" podcast.
